How it works

Not just a number. A better estimate.

QueueSense replaces messy paper waitlists and guessing games with our transparent TableFit estimation engine.

1

QR Check-In

Guests scan a restaurant QR code or join online. Staff can also instantly add walk-in parties manually.

2

Transparent Ranges

We show realistic ranges (e.g. 15–25 mins) rather than falsely precise promises that frustrate diners.

3

Live Queue Status

Customers track their exact position on their phone and get notified instantly when their table is ready.

4

Table-Aware Logic

Estimates adapt dynamically based on party size, available table inventory, and recent turn times.
